
Customer Segment Analysis (access date: 2025-10-22)

Summary of typical SMB customer segments and platform fit

- Shopify
  - Strong fit for DTC brands and growing SMBs aiming for omnichannel, with an extensive app ecosystem and performant checkout (Shop Pay). B2B on Shopify (Advanced/Plus) supports price lists, company profiles, net terms—suitable for upper-SMB wholesalers. Early SMBs benefit from fast time-to-value; watch app/TCO and third‑party gateway fees if not using Shopify Payments.

- WooCommerce
  - Best for content-led and cost-sensitive SMBs embedded in WordPress. Flexibility and ownership are key benefits; merchants orchestrate features via extensions (payments, tax, shipping, SEO). Operational maturity is required to manage hosting, security, and plugin maintenance. In‑person programs (M2, WisePad 3, Tap to Pay) exist; per‑transaction in‑person pricing isn’t centrally published.

- BigCommerce
  - Well-suited to catalog-heavy SMBs and scale-ups that value native multi‑storefront, solid product/variant support, and partner ecosystem. B2B Edition (add‑on) opens an upper‑SMB/mid‑market path with company accounts, quotes, and price lists. No platform transaction fees; payment processor rates are negotiated.

- Squarespace
  - Ideal for creators/solopreneurs and service-led SMBs who need elegant design, content tools, and simple commerce. Commerce tiers reduce processing and platform transaction fees as you upgrade; advanced shipping features are on Plus/Advanced. Less suited to complex B2B or deep catalog needs without partners.

- Wix
  - Suited to micro and DIY SMBs prioritizing ease-of-use and straightforward fees (e.g., Visa/Mastercard 2.9% + $0.30; AmEx 3.7% + $0.30 in the US). Robust editor, App Market, and automated tax via Avalara for eligible regions. Advanced OMS/B2B typically requires partners.

- Adobe Commerce (Magento)
  - Best for upper-SMB and mid-market with complex B2B, multi‑brand/multi‑store, and global expansion. Rich native B2B, multi‑store views, API depth (REST/GraphQL), and PWA options. Requires expert implementation and ongoing TCO planning; strong fit for manufacturers/distributors.

Signals that indicate segment fit

- App/extension reliance appetite (Shopify/Woo/Wix heavy; BigCommerce moderate; Adobe more native enterprise modules)
- Catalog complexity (variants/options, bundles, B2B price lists)
- Internationalization (markets, languages, duties/taxes)
- Omnichannel (POS maturity, channel breadth)
- Operating model (DIY vs partner-led; tolerance for hosting/maintenance)

Size band definitions
- Micro: <$1M GMV annually
- Lower SMB: $1M–$5M GMV
- SMB: $1M–$10M GMV
- Upper SMB: $10M–$25M GMV
- Mid‑market: $25M–$50M GMV

Time‑sensitive note: Fees and program availability (e.g., POS, payments, surcharges) vary by region and can change; this analysis reflects pages accessed on 2025-10-22.


Note on GMV ranges: These bands are heuristic conventions for SMB/mid‑market segmentation, not official platform thresholds.
