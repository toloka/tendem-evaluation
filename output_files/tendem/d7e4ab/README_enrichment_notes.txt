Data Enrichment: Restaurants Contact & Socials

Scope
- For each restaurant, collected and verified: Phone number (E.164 format), Website URL, main business Email (where available), and social profiles (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and other/X links placed under Other Social).

Sources & Verification Approach
- Primary sources: official restaurant websites/contact pages.
- Secondary corroboration: reputable listings (e.g., hotel partner pages, awards pages) and official social profiles.
- Cross-check: phone and website validated against at least two sources where possible.

Data Standards
- Phone: E.164 format (+44…).
- URLs: absolute URLs beginning with https:// or http://; for X/Twitter, links moved to Other Social; company TikTok links only (personal accounts removed).
- Empty/Unavailable fields: populated with N/A.

Quality Checks Performed
- Mandatory fields (Phone, Website) present for all entries.
- URL scheme and basic parseability validated across social fields.
- Duplicate social links across columns checked and none detected.
- Empty-string cells eliminated; N/A used consistently.

Notes
- The number column (№) is retained as the first column per client preference.
- Column order in deliverables: №, Restaurant, ADDRESS, Phone, Website, Email, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Other Social.
