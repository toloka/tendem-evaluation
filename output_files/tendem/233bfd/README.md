This document outlines the methodology used to compile the UK-wide list of high-end chauffeur and executive car hire companies.

**Methodology**

1.  **Initial Research & Discovery:**
    *   Targeted Google searches were performed for key UK cities and regions (e.g., “chauffeur services London,” “luxury chauffeur Manchester”) to identify premium operators.
    *   Online directories (Yell, Yelp, Addtoevent, Cylex) and Google Maps were used to supplement search results and find additional companies.
    *   Company websites were reviewed to confirm premium positioning (e.g., focus on Mercedes S-Class, Bentley, Rolls-Royce) and exclude non-qualifying services (taxis, minicabs, ride-hailing apps).

2.  **Data Enrichment & Verification:**
    *   Each company’s official website was visited to extract mandatory details: email, phone, base city, service types, vehicle fleet, and any notes on coverage or pricing.
    *   Where websites lacked a postal headquarters, Companies House records were used to source registered office postcodes, which were annotated in the Notes column for clarity.
    *   Reputable online directories and Google Maps listings were used to corroborate contact details and find addresses/postcodes when not available on the official site.
    *   Social media profiles (Facebook and Instagram) were searched for and added where an official, active page could be confidently identified.

3.  **Data Cleaning & Standardization:**
    *   A final duplicate check was performed to ensure each company was listed only once.
    *   Phone numbers were standardized to the +44 international format.
    *   Vehicle names were normalized for consistency (e.g., “Mercedes S-Class”).
    *   Social media links were formatted as full profile URLs.

**Sources Used:**

*   Google Search
*   Google Maps
*   Company Websites
*   Companies House (for registered office postcodes)
*   Yell, Yelp, Addtoevent, Cylex, and other reputable online directories
*   LinkedIn (for business activity confirmation, where needed)
