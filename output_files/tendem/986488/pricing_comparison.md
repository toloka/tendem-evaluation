Free Plan Availability and Limitations
- Monday.com (Free): Up to 2 seats; up to 3 boards and 3 docs; 8 column types; mobile apps; 200+ templates. No guest access; limited automations/integrations.
- Asana Personal (Free): Up to 10 teammates; unlimited tasks/projects/messages; 100MB/file; list/board/calendar views; mobile apps; 100+ integrations.
- ClickUp Free Forever: 60MB storage; unlimited tasks; unlimited free plan members; Kanban/Calendar views; 1 form; collaborative docs; 24/7 support.
- Notion Free: Unlimited for individuals; limits apply for 2+ members; 5MB/file; basic web publishing and forms; Notion Calendar/Mail included; trial of Notion AI.
- Smartsheet: No ongoing full-featured free tier for new users; 30-day trial available; unlimited free viewers; editors require paid licenses.

Notable Pricing Mechanics and Add-ons
- Monday.com: Seat minimums for paid plans; automations/integrations caps by tier; enterprise increases caps significantly.
- Asana: No 1-user paid plans; seat tiers start at 2 seats; AI Studio available (Basic included; Plus/Pro paid); portfolio/workload/time tracking on Advanced.
- ClickUp: AI and other add-ons available; guest seat allowances vary by plan; billing per Workspace.
- Notion: AI capabilities span tiers; custom domains for Sites incur fees; annual discount available; guests free but page-scoped.
- Smartsheet: Annual discount vs monthly; Business requires 3+ members; enterprise suites add governance/connectors.
