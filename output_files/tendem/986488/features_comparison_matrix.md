## Plan gating notes (minimum tier)
- Monday.com: Timeline/Gantt (Standard+); Time tracking (Pro+); Portfolio mgmt & Resource mgmt (Enterprise); Automations/Integrations action caps scale by plan.
- Asana: Forms (Starter+; branching/customization in Advanced); Portfolios/Goals (Advanced+); Workload and native Time tracking (Advanced+); Salesforce/Tableau/Power BI integrations (Advanced+); Enterprise+: EKM/Data residency/SIEM/DLP options.
- ClickUp: Gantt/Dashboards/Integrations (Unlimited+); Workload (Business+); SSO (Business+); Enterprise adds SCIM/data residency/custom roles; AI available across plans.
- Notion: Plus adds unlimited uploads/blocks/charts; Business adds SAML SSO, granular DB permissions, private teamspaces, premium integrations; Enterprise adds audit log, DLP/SIEM, domain management; Notion AI features vary by tier, Enterprise has zero data retention.
- Smartsheet: Pro has core views and 250 automations/month; Business adds unlimited automations, Timeline view, Team workload tracking, 1TB storage; Enterprise adds SSO/SAML, Work Insights, Collections; Advanced Work Management includes Control Center, Dynamic View, Data Shuttle, connectors.
