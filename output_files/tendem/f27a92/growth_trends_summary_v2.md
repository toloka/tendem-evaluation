
Growth Trend Analysis (last ~24 months) — access date 2025-10-22

Highlights:
- Shopify: Full-year 2024 revenue reached ~$8.9B, up 26% YoY; Q4 revenue growth accelerated to 31% YoY (press release). Signals consistent top-line expansion and operating leverage.
- BigCommerce: FY2024 revenue $332.9M (+8% YoY); Q4 revenue $87M (+3% YoY). Indicates modest growth and stabilization, with guidance pointing to continued improvement.
- Wix: Q4 2024 revenue $460.5M (+14% YoY); bookings up 18% YoY, reflecting solid demand across Business Solutions and Websites segments.
- Squarespace: ARRR grew 20% YoY to ~$1.18B in 2024; press coverage cites surpassing $1B annual revenue (+17% YoY). Trajectory suggests healthy subscription/commerce adoption and Payments expansion.
- WooCommerce: StoreLeads timeline shows large installed base; recent 2025 Q3 shows a slight -0.2% QoQ decrease globally. Over the last two years, WooCommerce remains one of the largest ecosystems by store count, with regional dynamics.
- Adobe Commerce (Magento): Positioned for B2B/B2C mid-market/enterprise growth; quantitative GMV/revenue metrics typically company-specific and not directly reported at platform level.

Notes:
- Figures reflect cited sources; fiscal calendars differ across companies.
- GMV metrics were not consistently disclosed in recent sources above; revenue/bookings/ARR proxies used where appropriate.
- Installed-base data (e.g., StoreLeads) measures detected stores, not GMV share; universe and methodology vary.
